System & Process for Donor Management & Communication

Hindu Fund’s donor engagement systems are designed to prioritise transparency, clear communication, and sustained connection. All interested supporters are onboarded through Zoho CRM and integrated into the Shubhchintak community, where they receive consistent updates on initiatives and outcomes.

Digital contributions are automatically acknowledged with receipts and personalised messages, and grant allocations are displayed on the website for real-time visibility—enabling donors to independently track fund utilisation with grantees and reinforcing trust across the ecosystem.

Process Flow - Donor Management

Lead Captured (Website / Event / Contact)
Added to Zoho CRM (Profile + Basic Information Stored)
Shubhchintak Community Inclusion (Regular Updates, Campaigns, Messages)
Donation Made via Website / Link
Auto-Receipt Generated · Personalized Thank You Email
Tracking of Donation Allocation (Visible on Website + Verification with Grantee)
Ongoing Relationship & Engagement (Impact Reports, Invites, Festive Greetings)

Our Priority Projects

Hindu Fund’s strategic focus remains on Manuscript Digitisation and Legal Advocacy, two key pillars supporting the long-term revival of Hindu civilisation. Additionally, we are also supporting Sanatan Gharvapsi Foundation.

1. MIDF — Manuscript & Inscription Digitisation Foundation
MIDF works to preserve, digitise, and make accessible India’s manuscripts, inscriptions, and oral knowledge archives, in collaboration with initiatives such as eGangotri and eSahitya Documentation Forum, ensuring civilisational memory endures for future generations.

2. Legal Advocacy
Hindu Fund backs critical Hindu legal battles to safeguard temples and sacred spaces, challenge discriminatory policies, and uphold fair governance.

3. Sanatan Gharwapsi Foundation
The Sanatan Gharwapsi Foundation (SGF) works to enable lawful and dignified return to Sanatan Dharma by providing structured legal, cultural, and community support across India.

Appeal

Hindu Fund is strengthening the financial foundation of Bharat’s civilizational resurgence by supporting institutions, scholars, and grassroots initiatives committed to upholding Dharma nationwide.

Through focused support across education, media, legal advocacy, and heritage preservation, each grant advances the Hindu narrative and enables changemakers to protect civilizational assets and drive spiritual and cultural renewal.
